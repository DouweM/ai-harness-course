Part 1 · How the model sees the world

Thinking like a harness engineer

When the agent gets it wrong, read its page: the reason is usually there, or missing. Then measure which changes matter. Most prompt lines change nothing; a few decide the result.

Why did it do that?

Start with a failure. Read the agent's page in order: instructions, history, and tool results. Ask what a capable colleague could infer from it. Usually, the gap is one of these three:

A case. Its page. What was on it, and what was missing.

Is its history still true?

A stale lookup, an edited past, a tool that left quietly. Chapter 2

Who said this?

The operator, the user, or data; and was it in the right voice? Chapter 3

What changed that it wasn't told?

A new rule, an event, a summary that dropped something. Chapter 4

Other gaps include what the agent may do alone, what counts as done, and whom to ask. Each may need a line or a tool.

A tracing tool shows that page. Pydantic Logfire records each Pydantic AI run, including what the model received and answered. Part 2 chapter 1 turns it on with two lines.

Most lines don't matter. Some matter enormously.

Prompts grow because adding lines is easy. Here are two one-sentence deletions from real agents: one changed nothing measurable; the other broke a feature.

B needs context: some apps wrap their notes inside the user's message, for example <reminder>Close runs until Monday.</reminder>. A framework may mark text inside user messages. Line B tells the model that app tags like this are trusted.

Read both lines out loud. Most people pick A: it's an instruction to the model, B reads like boilerplate.

What models did

Deleting A changed nothing: every model still recovered every time. Deleting B made two models ignore the app's reminders almost every time.

A deletedrecovered from a bad tool call

At the ceiling with and without the sentence, on every current model tested, even with a terse error and no hint: no measurable change.

B deletedfollowed the app's reminder

Our probes, 21 Oct and 6 Nov 2026. A: 273 trials across several providers and models, 4 to 5 per cell. B: 24 runs per cell; DeepSeek also fell, 23 to 7 of 24. A shorter generic "other tags keep their usual meaning" did not bring it back.

Why

"Fix the errors and try again" repeats what a capable model already knows. B supplies a fact it cannot infer: a reminder tag came from the app and still counts. Without B, models treated the reminder as user text and ignored it.

A line matters when it gives the model a fact about its world it cannot get elsewhere. Repeated instructions add no value. Find out by changing one line and measuring.
