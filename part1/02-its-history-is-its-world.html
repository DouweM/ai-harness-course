Part 1 · How the model sees the world

Its history is its world

An agent believes its history completely. When the history is slightly wrong, the agent is confidently wrong. Keep the story true: add to it, never quietly change it.

Monday 23 NovDana's payment for INV-2041 arrived on Friday. Your agent's history still holds Thursday's lookup: "open". And at month-end your app switched off the payment-posting tool for a few hours. Neither change is in any message.

From assistant to agent

Give the assistant from chapter 1 tools and a job, and it becomes an agent: it looks things up, acts, and reads the results, request after request. Every one of those calls and results stays on its page. That history is the agent's picture of the world, and nothing else is.

Its history is the only world it has.

Our probe, 2 Nov 2026. A support agent whose tool disappeared between turns; same shape as above. The six were Sonnet 5.5, Gemini 3.8 Flash, GPT-6 Luna, Kimi K2.5, Qwen3 Next and Mistral Small; gpt-oss-120b was 4 of 8, and 8 of 8 when the line listed every tool.

Why

The page is self-consistent: post_payment worked, and nobody said it stopped. To notice, Tok would have to infer a change from an absence, from a list that's merely shorter than one it read many requests ago. When the world changes and the page doesn't say so, Tok acts on the old world with full confidence. Telling it costs one line.

From where Tok sits

Lost a tool? I don't think so. It worked last time. Nobody said anything.

Edit the past, delete it, or add to it?

Monday, Dana writes: "did you get our payment?". Thursday's lookup in the history still says "open". Your harness can leave it, edit it to match, append a dated note, delete it to save tokens, or swap it for a placeholder. Each choice gives Tok a different past, and Tok will believe whichever one you give it. Try them.

A hole it has to fill

When the picture has a hole and the answer form leaves no room for "I don't know", the agent fills it with the most likely thing. Asked for a required due_date for "this Friday", with nothing on the page saying what today is:

due_date required, no date on the pagewhat came back

Our run, 15 Nov 2026. 5 runs per model. With the date optional, every model left it empty (15 of 15); with the arrival date on the page, every model wrote 20 November (15 of 15). Invented dates are the dangerous kind: they look exactly like real answers.

Keep the story true

The point

Tok's history is its world, and it trusts that world completely. When the world and the history disagree, the agent acts on the history, with full confidence. So add to the story instead of changing it: a dated note when a fact goes stale, a stub where you remove a result, one line when a tool leaves, and room in the answer for "I don't know".
