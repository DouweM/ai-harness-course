Part 1 · How the model sees the world

Its history is its world

An agent believes its history completely. When the history is slightly wrong, the agent is confidently wrong. Keep the story true: add to it, never quietly change it.

Skipped chapter 1? A model reads one page per request (its instructions, the tools it may call, the conversation so far) and continues it; it remembers nothing else. From assistant to agent

An assistant becomes an agent when given tools and a job: it asks your code to run tools, reads results, and chooses the next step. Calls and results stay on its page. That history is its picture of the world.

Its history is the only world it has.

The six at 7 or 8 of 8 were Sonnet 5.5 (both setups), Gemini 3.8 Flash, GPT-6 Luna, Kimi K2.5, Qwen3 Next and Mistral Small; gpt-oss-120b was 4 of 8, and 8 of 8 when the line listed every tool.

Why

The page is consistent: mark_paid worked, and nobody said it stopped. Tok would have to infer a change from a shorter tool list it saw many requests ago. When the world changes and the page doesn't say so, Tok acts on the old world with full confidence. One line can explain the change.

A hole it has to fill

Models predict, so they fill any open slot with their best guess. If a required due_date asks for "this Friday" and the page gives no date, the model guesses:

due_date required, no date on the pagewhat came back

Our run, 15 Nov 2026. 5 runs per model. With an optional date, every model left it empty (15 of 15); with the arrival date on the page, every model wrote 20 November (15 of 15). A required date missing from the email gets guessed, and looks real.

Give the answer a place for doubt. With a list on the answer form to write doubts in, models flagged a contradictory email for a person to check in 30 of 30 runs; with a confidence score instead, in 0 of 30. A model writes 95 on almost anything.

Our run, 15 Nov 2026. A promise for Friday plus a line claiming the invoice was settled last week, 10 runs per model per arm on Sonnet 5.5, GPT-6.1 Sol and Gemini 3.8 Flash. Every doubts list on the form named the contradiction; GPT and Gemini scored the email 95 to 100, the same as a clean promise. On clean promises no model invented a doubt (0 of 15).

Keep the story true

The point

Tok trusts its history completely. When the world and history disagree, the agent acts on the history with full confidence. Add a dated note when a fact goes stale, a stub when removing a result, one line when a tool leaves, and room in the answer for "I don't know".
