The loop

The three parts form a loop. Find what the model is missing, build an idea, measure it on your cases, and use misses to design the next change.

One loop, three steps

Each dot is a chapter; the filled dot is this one. Tap a part to send Tok there.

Each step needs the others: an untested idea is an opinion, an unmeasured change only shows it runs, and an eval without an idea says what changed but not why. One trip is a lap. Most improve an existing agent; the best version may combine separately tested ideas. Building from scratch is a first lap to compare directions.

Two laps, from this course

  1. Design. A compaction summary dropped the billing team's rule ("never contact a debtor before 9am their time"). Every model broke it, 80 of 80 (Part 1, chapter 4). Idea: flag that the summary may be incomplete.
  2. Build. Append one sentence to the summary and make the full history searchable.
  3. Measure. GPT-6.1 Sol and Gemini 3.8 Flash kept the rule by checking full history, 39 of 40 across two wordings; Claude Sonnet and Haiku 5.5, 0 of 40. Half a fix.
  4. Next lap. Keep user rules verbatim in the summary instead of relying on the model to notice. Add a summary_prompt to TieredCompaction (Part 2, chapter 4), beside billing_world in chapter 6. Across the same four models and 10 runs each, all kept the rule, 40 of 40.

Build in the second lap: it worked on every model. The first showed which model families notice gaps and shaped the fix.

A dropped rule, half a fix, a split by family, then a fix on every model.

A lap on your own agent

With traces and corrected cases, a first lap can take a day:

  1. Pick one decision people correct, and export 20 or more corrections.
  2. Write an evaluator for the costly mistake.
  3. Run the current version as arm A and read every miss.
  4. Form one idea from the misses: what can't it see, whose voice is it, or what moved?
  5. Implement one change, ideally one capability, and run it as arm B.
  6. Compare and record B's improvement by case, what to ship, what to watch online, and the next lap.

You need the agent's code, access to traces or corrections, and a model API key. Your coding agent can type; "Ask your agent" below gives it this chapter.

One lap on an agent they already run. Ask the room which decision of their agent gets corrected most.
