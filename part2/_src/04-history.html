History

Part 1 said its history is its world. In code, that history is a list you store and pass back, and when it gets too long, how you make room decides what the model still knows.

Tuesday to Thursday, stored

The point: you own the list. Store the whole conversation; when it gets long, clear tool results with a stub instead of cutting messages. The prediction shows what cutting loses.

What Friday's page holds

Cut to the last four, the page opens on a tool call the model made with no request before it, which some providers reject outright, and the model no longer knows the payment is split: Friday's 2,060 looks like a short payment. Cleared, every message stays, and the old lookups become a note saying how to get them back.

Offline, deterministic. Both pages are what Pydantic AI 2.68 and the Harness 0.68 actually send. How models treat a missing result is measured: whether it was deleted or replaced by a stub, four hosted models looked the fact up again, 160 times out of 160; deleting without a stub also made Sonnet 5.5 disown its own earlier answers (Part 1 chapter 2). A smaller open-weight model is another story; Part 3 chapter 2 measures it.

Why

Tool results are most of a long agent's context and the cheapest thing to drop: the agent can fetch them again. The customer's words are the opposite: small, and impossible to fetch again. Make room from the side that can be rebuilt.

From where Tok sits

A page in my logbook that says "cleared, look it up again" is fine. A logbook that starts in the middle is not.

When clearing isn't enough: compaction

TieredCompaction is the Harness's recommended default: it clears tool results first and only summarizes older turns with a model call when clearing doesn't free enough. A summary is the one step that can drop what the customer said, so tell the summarizer what to keep. In Part 1 chapter 4 a summary that dropped the user's rule led every model to break it, 80 times out of 80.

What you can do now

Find where your agent trims history. Replace cutting with clearing old tool results, give the cleared result a note that says how to get it back, and write your summary prompt around what your customers say.
