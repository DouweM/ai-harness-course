Instructions and deps

Standing rules go in instructions=. Facts that change go in deps, and an instructions function writes them onto the page every request. A fact frozen into a string goes stale without anyone noticing.

The date that froze

The point: facts from your app belong in deps and an instructions function, not in an f-string at import. The prediction has real stakes: a promise recorded for a Friday that already passed.

Offline, deterministic. A FunctionModel stands in for the model and works out "this Friday" from the date on its page, the way models did in Part 1. Use the date the email arrived, a fact your code knows, rather than today's: a queue that works through yesterday's mail would pick the wrong week.

When a value changes mid-conversation

An instructions function runs before every request, so its line is always current. For a value that can change while a conversation is open, like month-end close starting on Thursday, give it a name and on_change='append': the old instructions stay as they were, and the change arrives at the end of the page, at the moment it happened. Part 1 chapter 4 showed why: rewriting the top left some models writing like their own past (Grok and Qwen 0 of 24), while appending the change worked on all six models tested (24 of 24). Chapter 3 uses this for a tool that leaves.

What you can do now

Grep your prompts for f-strings and .format(. Every interpolated fact becomes a deps field and an instructions function; every value that can change mid-conversation gets a name and on_change='append'.
