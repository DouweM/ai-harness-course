Capabilities: the unit of harness behavior

Everything chapters 2 to 5 built lives on one agent. A capability packages it once: instructions, tools, gates and hooks, attached to any agent. The built-ins, and whole agents like Coder, are made the same way.

Building blocks for your own

Three ways in, from least to most code. Capability(...) with decorators, as above. Hooks() with @hooks.on.<event>, for one lifecycle hook, like an audit log on the reminders agent only. And a subclass of AbstractCapability when the rule needs configuration, tests or a package of its own: here the close rule, as a class that hides every writing tool.

If you subclassed or wrapped an agent to add logging or guards, a Hooks capability replaces it, and works on every agent.
