The running example, in plain words

The course follows a billing agent that reads customer emails, records promises to pay, and records payments when money arrives. These terms explain the example.

The words

WordWhat it means here
InvoiceA bill sent to a customer, with a number like INV-2041, an amount, and a due date.
OverdueThe due date has passed and the invoice isn't paid yet.
Promise to payA customer saying when they will pay, for example "this Friday". The agent records the amount and date so the team can follow up if payment doesn't arrive.
WireA bank transfer. "Dana wires 2,060.00" means she sends that amount to the company's bank.
Bank feedThe bank's list of incoming payments. A wire appears first as pending, then confirmed.
LedgerThe company's record of invoices and payments: what each customer owes and what has arrived.
Recording a payment (record_payment)Recording money received against an invoice. A partial payment leaves the invoice open for the rest.
Marking an invoice paid (mark_paid)Closing an invoice as fully paid. Because this changes what the company thinks it is owed, a person must approve it.
Month-end closeA few days at month end when finance checks and freezes the books. Invoice statuses cannot change during close, so the app removes mark_paid from the agent until it ends.
DisputeA customer says an invoice is wrong, such as its amount. A person handles it; the agent doesn't settle it.
ApprovalA person checks a write before it happens. The run stops while the person decides.
The billing app, the billing teamThe company's software and its users, who ask the agent to do things. Dana is the customer; a tool reads her emails as data.

Dana's week

Dana is a customer whose company owes 4,120.00 on invoice INV-2041.

  • Dana promises to pay INV-2041, then clarifies that she will pay half (2,060.00) first and the rest by the 30th.
  • She wires the first half; the bank confirms it, and the ledger records it after approval. INV-2041 remains open for the balance.
  • A question about the invoice amount becomes a dispute for a person. The agent also marks a different invoice, INV-2038, paid.

INV-2041 is not marked paid because only half has been paid.

What actually happens in the examples

The examples never touch a real bank or ledger. When a result says the agent "marked an invoice paid" or "sent an email", the model requested that tool call and the offline code recorded it. In your system, the same call would make a real change, so some calls require approval.
